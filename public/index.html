Futures Trading
📚 How Futures Work — click to learn
What is a futures contract?
A futures contract is an agreement to profit (or lose) based on the price movement of an asset — like a stock index, oil, or gold. You don't own the asset. You're simply betting on whether the price will go up or down.
📈 LONG — betting price rises
You open a Long position when you think a price will go up.
Example: You go Long 5 contracts of ES=F (S&P 500) at $5,000.
If it rises to $5,050 → Profit = 5 × ($5,050 − $5,000) = +$250
If it falls to $4,980 → Loss = 5 × ($4,980 − $5,000) = −$100
📉 SHORT — betting price falls
You open a Short position when you think a price will go down.
Example: You go Short 3 contracts of CL=F (Crude Oil) at $80.
If it falls to $75 → Profit = 3 × ($80 − $75) = +$15
If it rises to $85 → Loss = 3 × ($80 − $85) = −$15
💰 Margin — your deposit
You only put up a margin deposit (e.g. 20% of position value) — not the full amount. This cash is locked while the position is open and returned when you close it, plus or minus your P&L.
Example: 10 contracts at $5,000, 20% margin = $10,000 reserved. You still profit/lose on all 10 contracts.
🔢 P&L Formula
Long: P&L = contracts × (exit price − entry price)
Short: P&L = contracts × (entry price − exit price)

When you close a position, your margin is returned plus your realized profit (or minus your loss). Unrealized P&L is included in your total portfolio value while the position is open.
🎮 How to trade here
1. Click a contract below to select it
2. Choose LONG or SHORT
3. Enter how many contracts you want
4. Use the P&L Simulator to preview outcomes
5. Click Open Position
6. Monitor in your Portfolio tab
7. Click Close in Portfolio to take profit/loss
